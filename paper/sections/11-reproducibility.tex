\section{Verification and availability}\label{sec:repro}

The sources, exact certificates and Lean development are available at \repo. The classification, witness constructions and asymptotic bounds have analytic proofs in the paper. The finite threshold claims in Proposition~\ref{prop:paritythresholds} also depend on rational primal and dual certificates, checked with integer and rational arithmetic and Sturm sequences. Figure~\ref{fig:thresholds} plots the certified endpoints. The triangle brackets at orders $11$ to $13$ quoted after Proposition~\ref{prop:parityconversion} rest on certificates of the same kind. The exact checks of the cubic certificates of Remark~\ref{rem:cubicorder} on laws of random finite models are falsification tests; we prove the certificates in the text, and the proofs do not depend on these checks. Appendix~\ref{app:certificates} describes the formats and finite coverage; the repository's \texttt{paper/review/VERIFICATION\_GUIDE.md} gives replay commands and checker details.

The Lean development proves the binary classification equivalence and existence of strictly positive witnesses for finite latent alphabets, including the structural lemmas, double-star reconstruction, cycle and five-path obstructions, their distance lower bounds and local flips. Rationality of the classification witnesses follows from the finite constructions in the paper; the mapped Lean statements do not assert it. The terminal declarations for the corrected square and triangle witnesses prove the case $q=1/(16t)$ of Theorems~\ref{thm:squarelinear} and~\ref{thm:trianglelinear}, with the constants $c=5$ and $c=4$ in the density; the theorems in the paper cover $0<q\le(16/15)^{2/t}-1$ and $0<q\le(9/8)^{2/t}-1$ with $c=mR^{m-1}$. The passage to arbitrary latent spaces uses the cardinality theorem of~\cite{RGW2018}, which is not formalized here. A separate triangle development proves the defect construction and nontermination for arbitrary latent probability spaces and measurable stochastic responses, the fan bounds, and the finite bounds on the defect family. It also proves the triangle norm estimates from Theorem~\ref{thm:convexorder}.

The general convex-order inequality, the corrected densities beyond $q=1/(16t)$, the max-moment estimate and survivor corollaries, order conversion, count-moment and threshold results including Proposition~\ref{prop:parityconversion}, the cubic certificates and rejecting orders of Remark~\ref{rem:cubicorder}, distance asymptotics, bit-length theorem and supplementary inequalities are not formalized. Neither are the larger-observed-alphabet transfer or the limiting statements for the defect family. Declaration-level coverage appears in \texttt{README.md} and \texttt{formalization.yaml}; the axiom audit lists $113$ declarations.

The binary, finite-latent classification is registered as \texttt{PALOMAR-2026-09-14-000009}, version~1, at the \href{https://palomar-registry.org/entry.html?id=PALOMAR-2026-09-14-000009&version=1}{Palomar registry}. That registration concerns the stated classification theorem, not all results in this paper.
