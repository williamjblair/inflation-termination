\section{Reproducibility}\label{sec:repro}

The proofs do not depend on computation. The accompanying artifact contains exact rational certificates and checkers for the following finite instances:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item structural certificates at orders $t=1,2,3$: the root law, the line supports \eqref{eq:outputs}, and the target atoms of $P_t$ as exact rationals; the checker verifies nonnegativity and normalization, covariance of the supports under every adjacent-transposition generator of $S_t^3$, all $t^3$ copied-triangle marginals by exact enumeration over the relevant roots, mutual disjointness of the diagonal supports $R_\ell$, all $8^t$ diagonal equations against $P_t^{\otimes t}$, and the exact Finner gap;
\item a sparse full order-two inflation table with $166$ nonzero coordinates out of $2^{12}$ and denominator $2^{32}$, produced by a separate enumeration of all $256$ defect configurations; the checker treats it as an untrusted linear-programming vector and verifies normalization, nonnegativity, all $12{,}288$ generator-coordinate symmetry equations and all $64$ diagonal equations;
\item a negative control: the same table with one denominator unit moved from the all-one to the all-zero atom, which is normalized and nonnegative and is rejected by the diagonal equations;
\item an exact rational order-two Farkas certificate rejecting $R_{1/2}$ from Proposition~\ref{prop:Rp};
\item fan certificates: for fourteen members $P_\eps$, $\eps=k^{-3}$, the exact atoms, the Bernoulli passing certificate at order $1+k/2$, the exact fan value $v_t$ at the first rejecting order of the fan inequality, and the second-moment rejecting order; an all-order certificate of the pointwise inequality \eqref{eq:fan-pointwise} as a nonnegative polynomial identity in the count $S$, checked exhaustively over $174{,}760$ assignments and $271{,}500$ count orbits; the $2{,}660$ index-permutation embeddings behind \eqref{eq:fan-cross}; and eight rejected negative controls. The checker uses explicit exceptions and passes identically under an optimized interpreter.
\item classification certificates (\texttt{artifact/certificates/classification/}): for the cycles $C_3,C_4,C_5$ at orders one and two, the five-vertex path at orders one and two, the square parity witness of Theorem~\ref{thm:squarelinear} at orders one and two, and the triangle witness of Theorem~\ref{thm:trianglelinear} at orders one to three, each with and without the flips that make the target strictly positive, the verifier reconstructs the global witness from the density formula and checks normalization and positivity of every atom, invariance under every copy-index permutation, the full diagonal law, every injectable marginal and every ancestral-independence product (all $18{,}054$ families at path order two and all $26{,}671$ at $C_5$ order two), the incompatibility of each target (parity rigidity on the cycles, the bilocal inequality on the path, a max-moment inequality on the square and on the triangle), and rejection of a negative control with one unit of mass moved; at triangle order three, where the table has $2^{27}$ atoms, the diagonal law and the $15{,}850$ prescriptions are checked by exact Fourier characters rather than atom by atom; the minimum of the corrected densities of Lemma~\ref{lem:corrected} is scanned exactly over all assignments up to $t=5$ for the square and $t=6$ for the triangle; for Proposition~\ref{prop:paritythresholds}(iv) at order two, count-form primal witnesses at $q=3/20$ (order two, Navascu\'es--Wolfe) and $q=1/10$ (order two, ancestral independence) expanded to full tables of $2^{16}$ atoms, the order-two dual with its polynomial identity $\E D=(1+q)g(q)$ verified coefficient by coefficient over the $81$ count configurations, and the order-three dual over $256$ configurations at $q=1/10$.
\item parity-threshold certificates (\texttt{artifact/certificates/exponent/}): for the square and the triangle, for each hierarchy and each order $t\le10$, a rational count witness at the lower endpoint of Table~\ref{tab:thresholds}, checked against every moment prescribed by Lemmas~\ref{lem:countmoment} and~\ref{lem:countmomentNW}, and a chain of rational dual functionals with Sturm certificates of positivity on intervals covering everything above the upper endpoint; the degree sets are enumerated by brute force for $t\le3$ and every ancestral-independence degree tuple is realized by an explicit family checked against the definitions for $t\le10$; the verifier runs $20{,}004$ exact checks in under a minute. The count-moment reduction itself and Lemma~\ref{lem:paritymonotone} are proved in the text and assumed by the verifier.
\end{itemize}
The certified first rejecting orders of $P_\eps$ for $\eps=k^{-3}$ are $4,5,6,7,10$ at $k=4,6,8,10,16$, where the passing and fan bounds meet; for $k=32,128,1024,65536$ the fan bounds are $19,70,551,35122$ against lower bounds on the first rejecting order of $18,66,514,32770$. The exact Finner gaps at $t=1,2,3$ are $1/8$, $9273825/2^{32}$ and $327279338388988503553/1792720717930698493280256$, above the uniform bounds $\eps_t^2/2=1/8$, $1/512$, $1/5832$. The certificate checkers use Python integers and exact fractions, without a linear-programming solver or floating-point comparisons. The hardened nontermination checker raises an explicit exception on every failed check and refuses to run under an optimized interpreter; the artifact keeps the original checker unchanged for provenance and includes mutation tests (truncated, empty, corrupted, wrong-order and unsymmetric inputs, and optimized-interpreter runs) with their logs. A manifest with SHA-256 identities and a fresh replay record with operating system, interpreter version, commands, wall times and output hashes accompany the artifact.

To replay the checks, use Python 3 from the directory containing \texttt{paper/} and \texttt{artifact/}:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 -B artifact/verifiers/run_replay.py
python3 -B artifact/verifiers/mutation_tests.py
python3 -B artifact/verifiers/mutation_tests_fan.py
python3 -B artifact/certificates/classification/verify_classification.py
python3 -B artifact/certificates/exponent/verify_exponent.py
\end{verbatim}
The replay runner copies each certificate set into a temporary directory and writes new records under \texttt{artifact/replay\_logs/}. Its independent distance check requires \texttt{mpmath}; the exact certificate checkers use the Python standard library. Replaying changes the log files, so the supplied manifest identifies the distributed snapshot, not regenerated logs. The finite checks verify the listed tables and certificates; the all-order and distance-asymptotic claims rest on the proofs above.

\paragraph{Formal verification.}
Theorem~\ref{thm:main} and the results it rests on are formalized in Lean~4 with Mathlib: Lemma~\ref{lem:finner} for arbitrary probability spaces and measurable stochastic responses; the defect-cube law with its symmetry, diagonal law, injectable marginals and ancestral products (Lemmas~\ref{lem:disjoint}, \ref{lem:triangle-law}, \ref{lem:symmetry} and~\ref{lem:diag}, and Theorem~\ref{thm:membership} for $\IAI_t\subseteq\INW_t$); the margin of Lemma~\ref{lem:violation}; Appendix~\ref{app:injectable}; Theorem~\ref{thm:fan} with the rejecting-order bound; Proposition~\ref{prop:Rp}; the finite bounds of Proposition~\ref{prop:family}; the triangle case of Proposition~\ref{prop:promised}; and the $\ell^2$ form \eqref{eq:convexl2} of Theorem~\ref{thm:convexorder} for the triangle with the bound $\sqrt7/(2\sqrt n)$ of Corollary~\ref{cor:brackets}. The main statement is \texttt{no\_finite\_characterizing\_orderM}, whose compatible set allows arbitrary latent probability spaces; the earlier finite-latent version is kept alongside it. These modules contain no \texttt{sorry}, and every theorem depends only on the axioms \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}.

The pair-source results of Sections~\ref{sec:pairsource} to~\ref{sec:fivepath} are formalized over a graph-structure development whose definitions are proved to agree with the triangle module on the triangle. Proved there, with binary observed variables and finite latent alphabets throughout: the three tests with their nesting; soundness (Proposition~\ref{prop:gsound}); the trail criterion and the root-sink lemma (Lemmas~\ref{lem:trail} and~\ref{lem:rootsink}); source-disjoint independence at order two (Lemma~\ref{lem:sourcedisjoint}); induced-subgraph transport (Lemma~\ref{lem:transport}); the exhaustion lemma (Lemma~\ref{lem:exhaustion}); the double-star reconstruction with its labelling step (Theorem~\ref{thm:doublestar} for binary alphabets, Corollary~\ref{cor:disjointunions}); the cycle target, its characters and exact parity rigidity (Lemmas~\ref{lem:parityaux} and~\ref{lem:parityrigidity}); the quantitative rigidity of Lemma~\ref{lem:quantrigidity}; the cycle witness at every order, its incompatibility and the distance bound (Lemmas~\ref{lem:cyclewitness} and~\ref{lem:cycledistance}); the positivity and moment table of the corrected density in the triangle case and the triangle witness at $q=1/(16t)$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:corrected} with $m=3$, Theorem~\ref{thm:trianglelinear}); the five-path target, the bilocal inequality, the distance bound and the witness at every order (Lemmas~\ref{lem:pathtarget} and~\ref{lem:bilocal}, Corollary~\ref{cor:pathdistance}, Theorem~\ref{thm:fivepath}); local flips; and Theorem~\ref{thm:classification} itself in the binary case, assembled from these (\texttt{classification\_NW}, \texttt{classification\_AI}, \texttt{classification\_exp}). The coverage map \texttt{lean/coverage/inflation-nontermination.json} lists the declarations against the statements. Not formalized: the square witness of Theorem~\ref{thm:squarelinear} (recorded as a statement with \texttt{sorry} in a separate library that the audited development does not import), Lemma~\ref{lem:maxmoment}, Corollaries~\ref{cor:squaredistance} and~\ref{cor:triangledistance}, the convex-order inequality for general convex functions, Theorem~\ref{thm:orderconversion}, Lemma~\ref{lem:countmomentNW}, Lemma~\ref{lem:paritymonotone}, Proposition~\ref{prop:paritythresholds}, the larger-alphabet transfer of Remark~\ref{rem:alphabets}, Theorem~\ref{thm:distance}, Proposition~\ref{prop:bits}, the limit form of Proposition~\ref{prop:family}, Corollary~\ref{cor:alphabets} and Appendix~\ref{app:supporting}. The Lean sources accompany the artifact.
