\section{Reproducibility}\label{sec:repro}

The proofs do not depend on computation. The accompanying artifact contains exact rational certificates and checkers for the following finite instances:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item structural certificates at orders $t=1,2,3$: the root law, the line supports \eqref{eq:outputs}, and the target atoms of $P_t$ as exact rationals; the checker verifies nonnegativity and normalization, covariance of the supports under every adjacent-transposition generator of $S_t^3$, all $t^3$ copied-triangle marginals by exact enumeration over the relevant roots, mutual disjointness of the diagonal supports $R_\ell$, all $8^t$ diagonal equations against $P_t^{\otimes t}$, and the exact Finner gap;
\item a sparse full order-two inflation table with $166$ nonzero coordinates out of $2^{12}$ and denominator $2^{32}$, produced by a separate enumeration of all $256$ defect configurations; the checker treats it as an untrusted linear-programming vector and verifies normalization, nonnegativity, all $12{,}288$ generator-coordinate symmetry equations and all $64$ diagonal equations;
\item a negative control: the same table with one denominator unit moved from the all-one to the all-zero atom, which is normalized and nonnegative and is rejected by the diagonal equations;
\item an exact rational order-two Farkas certificate rejecting $R_{1/2}$ from Proposition~\ref{prop:Rp};
\item fan certificates: for fourteen members $P_\eps$, $\eps=k^{-3}$, the exact atoms, the Bernoulli passing certificate at order $1+k/2$, the exact fan value $v_t$ at the first rejecting order of the fan inequality, and the second-moment rejecting order; an all-order certificate of the pointwise inequality \eqref{eq:fan-pointwise} as a nonnegative polynomial identity in the count $S$, checked exhaustively over $174{,}760$ assignments and $271{,}500$ count orbits; the $2{,}660$ index-permutation embeddings behind \eqref{eq:fan-cross}; and eight rejected negative controls. The checker uses explicit exceptions and passes identically under an optimized interpreter.
\end{itemize}
The certified first rejecting orders of $P_\eps$ for $\eps=k^{-3}$ are $4,5,6,7,10$ at $k=4,6,8,10,16$, where the passing and fan bounds meet; for $k=32,128,1024,65536$ the fan bounds are $19,70,551,35122$ against lower bounds on the first rejecting order of $18,66,514,32770$. The exact Finner gaps at $t=1,2,3$ are $1/8$, $9273825/2^{32}$ and $327279338388988503553/1792720717930698493280256$, above the uniform bounds $\eps_t^2/2=1/8$, $1/512$, $1/5832$. The certificate checkers use Python integers and exact fractions, without a linear-programming solver or floating-point comparisons. The hardened nontermination checker raises an explicit exception on every failed check and refuses to run under an optimized interpreter; the artifact keeps the original checker unchanged for provenance and includes mutation tests (truncated, empty, corrupted, wrong-order and unsymmetric inputs, and optimized-interpreter runs) with their logs. A manifest with SHA-256 identities and a fresh replay record with operating system, interpreter version, commands, wall times and output hashes accompany the artifact.

To replay the checks, use Python 3 from the directory containing \texttt{paper/} and \texttt{artifact/}:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 -B artifact/verifiers/run_replay.py
python3 -B artifact/verifiers/mutation_tests.py
python3 -B artifact/verifiers/mutation_tests_fan.py
\end{verbatim}
The replay runner copies each certificate set into a temporary directory and writes new records under \texttt{artifact/replay\_logs/}. Its independent distance check requires \texttt{mpmath}; the exact certificate checkers use the Python standard library. Replaying changes the log files, so the supplied manifest identifies the distributed snapshot, not regenerated logs. The finite checks verify the listed tables and certificates; the all-order and distance-asymptotic claims rest on the proofs above.

\paragraph{Formal verification.}
Theorem~\ref{thm:main} and the results it rests on are formalized in Lean~4 with Mathlib: the finite-alphabet form of Lemma~\ref{lem:finner}; the defect-cube law with its symmetry, diagonal law, injectable marginals and ancestral products (Lemmas~\ref{lem:disjoint}, \ref{lem:triangle-law}, \ref{lem:symmetry} and~\ref{lem:diag}, and Theorem~\ref{thm:membership} for $\IAI_t\subseteq\INW_t$); the margin of Lemma~\ref{lem:violation}; Appendix~\ref{app:injectable}; Theorem~\ref{thm:fan} with the rejecting-order bound; Proposition~\ref{prop:Rp}; the finite bounds of Proposition~\ref{prop:family}; and the triangle case of Proposition~\ref{prop:promised}. The headline statement is \texttt{TriangleInflation.no\_finite\_characterizing\_order}. The development contains no \texttt{sorry}, and every theorem depends only on the axioms \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}. Three boundaries are recorded with the sources. The recursively expressible hierarchy of Definition~\ref{def:exp} is not formalized, so the formal membership statements cover the two larger feasible sets. The formal compatible set uses finite latent alphabets, which by \cite{RGW2018} loses nothing but is not itself formalized, so the formal incompatibility statements are the weaker reading. Theorem~\ref{thm:distance}, Proposition~\ref{prop:bits} and the limit form of Proposition~\ref{prop:family} are not formalized. The Lean sources accompany the artifact.
