\section{Open problems}\label{sec:open}

\begin{question}[Worst-case convergence exponent]\label{q:hausdorff}
For the square and the triangle, Corollary~\ref{cor:brackets} gives $\Omega(1/t)\le H_t^H\le O(t^{-1/2})$ for each hierarchy $H$. What is the sharp asymptotic rate? Does a power-law exponent exist, and does the rate depend on the hierarchy?
\end{question}
For longer cycles Theorem~\ref{thm:cycle} gives $\Omega(m^{-2}t^{-2})$, and for the five-vertex path Theorem~\ref{thm:fivepath} gives $\Omega(t^{-2})$.

\begin{question}[Exact leading constant along the defect family]\label{q:constant}
Does $\eps^{1/3}\tmin^H(P_\eps)$ converge? If so, what is its limit, and do the three hierarchies share it?
\end{question}
The current bounds give the interval $[\tfrac12,4-2\sqrt3]$.

\begin{question}[Separation of the hierarchies]\label{q:separation}
Is the factor $3/2$ of Theorem~\ref{thm:orderconversion} sharp, and does a bounded order conversion hold on every pair-source scenario?
\end{question}
Proposition~\ref{prop:paritythresholds} gives finite-order separations. It remains open whether some fixed scenario satisfies $\IAI_t\subsetneq\INW_t$ at every order $t\ge2$.

\paragraph{Beyond pair-source scenarios (resolved).}\label{q:hypergraph}
The companion hypergraph manuscript~\cite{BlairHypergraphTermination2026} now closes the qualitative question under
an exact finite contract.  For every finite reduced classical scenario whose
observed alphabets are finite and have size at least two, each of NW, AI and
recursively expressible inflation has a finite characterizing order if and
only if the scenario is conformal and its primal graph is chordal,
$P_5$-free and double-gem-free.  Equivalently, the primal graph is chordal
recursively split-substitute.  Thus the three hierarchies have the same
finite/infinite topology class, although their least orders can differ.
Remark~\ref{rem:hypergraph} remains relevant: the ordinary observer graph does
not encode source conformality and therefore does not by itself decide the
classification.  The result is internally proved in the companion repository;
external referee acceptance and priority remain unresolved.

\begin{question}[Practical rejecting orders beyond supplied certificates]\label{q:effective}
Can one obtain sharp, practical and directly evaluable rejecting-order bounds
for every incompatible rational law on the triangle or square, including laws
that satisfy all Finner and cubic parity inequalities considered here, without
first supplying a quantitative separating certificate or invoking enormous
fixed-scenario real-algebraic preprocessing?
\end{question}
Theorem~\ref{thm:fan} covers the laws that violate a Finner inequality, and Remark~\ref{rem:cubicorder} covers the laws on the square and the triangle that violate a cubic parity certificate. The Spiral-inflation inequalities of~\cite[Section IV C]{WSF2019} and equation~(31) of~\cite{NW2020} are closed-form criteria that reject some Finner-satisfying laws at order two, but they give no rejecting order beyond a fixed level. The constants $8$ and $10$ of the certificates are not known to be sharp. The distance and bit-length bounds apply more broadly, but computing the distance or extracting the constants from quantifier elimination may be impractical.

The recursion-theoretic part of the former question is
closed~\cite{BlairEffectiveInflation2026}.  Exact
compatibility of a rational law in a fixed finite classical scenario is
decidable by finite-cardinality semialgebraic methods, so enumerating inflation
orders computes its first rejecting order (and returns zero on compatible
inputs).  Quantitative certificate promises give explicit bounds, including
the maintained binary-triangle regularized-KL and margin/curvature formulas.
For a fixed scenario and fixed finite alphabet profile, using the ordinary
dense numerator/denominator encoding of rational target probabilities, the
worst first-rejecting order at target bit length at most $B$ is bounded on a
terminating topology and is $2^{\Theta(B)}$ on a nonterminating topology, for
each of NW, AI and recursively expressible inflation.  This fixed-instance
proof-depth law is not a varying-scenario complexity bound.

\begin{question}[Quantum and no-signalling analogues]\label{q:quantum}
For which quantum or no-signalling inflation hierarchies can one construct incompatible laws with an unbounded first rejecting order and determine its growth?
\end{question}
