\section{Open problems}\label{sec:open}

\begin{question}[Worst-case convergence exponent]\label{q:hausdorff}
For the square and the triangle, Corollary~\ref{cor:brackets} gives $\Omega(1/t)\le H_t^H\le O(t^{-1/2})$ for each hierarchy $H$. Which exponent in $[1/2,1]$ is sharp, and does it depend on the hierarchy?
\end{question}
The lower bound uses one parity direction with the worst-case profile of Lemma~\ref{lem:maxmoment}. Along the parity direction itself the exponent is one for both hierarchies: the certified thresholds of Proposition~\ref{prop:paritythresholds} give $tq_t$ increasing towards about $0.42$ on the square and $0.93$ on the triangle through order ten, so any law accepted by the Navascu\'es--Wolfe test at distance of order $t^{-1/2}$ from the compatible set, if one exists, lies in another direction. The same question is open for longer cycles, where Theorem~\ref{thm:cycle} gives only $\Omega(m^{-2}t^{-2})$, and for the five-vertex path, where Theorem~\ref{thm:fivepath} gives $\Omega(t^{-2})$.

\begin{question}[Exact leading constant along the defect family]\label{q:constant}
Does $\eps^{1/3}\tmin^H(P_\eps)$ converge? If so, what is its limit, and do the three hierarchies share it?
\end{question}
The current bounds give the interval $[\tfrac12,4-2\sqrt3]$. The finite certificates determine the first rejecting order at $\eps=k^{-3}$ for $k\in\{4,6,8,10,16\}$; the passing and fan bounds differ at the larger tested values.

\begin{question}[Separation of the hierarchies]\label{q:separation}
Theorem~\ref{thm:orderconversion} shows that on the square the Navascués--Wolfe and ancestral-independence hierarchies differ by at most a factor $3/2$ in the order. Is the factor sharp, and does a bounded conversion hold on every pair-source scenario?
\end{question}
Along the parity direction the certified thresholds of Proposition~\ref{prop:paritythresholds} separate the two hierarchies at every even order up to ten and make them coincide at every odd order up to nine, on both the square and the triangle; we do not know whether the inclusion $\IAI_t\subseteq\INW_t$ is strict at every order on any scenario, nor why the parity direction alternates.

\begin{question}[Beyond pair-source scenarios]\label{q:hypergraph}
Which scenarios with a source shared by three or more observed variables admit a finite characterizing order?
\end{question}
The reconstruction of Theorem~\ref{thm:doublestar} uses that every source has two endpoints, and the exhaustion of Lemma~\ref{lem:exhaustion} is a statement about graphs. Both would need replacements for hypergraphs. The nontermination side transfers to any scenario that contains a non-double-star pair-source scenario as an induced substructure in the sense of Lemma~\ref{lem:transport}.

\begin{question}[Explicit rejecting orders beyond Finner]\label{q:effective}
Can one bound the rejecting order in directly evaluable quantities of an incompatible input law that satisfies every Finner inequality?
\end{question}
Theorem~\ref{thm:fan} covers Finner violations on the triangle and Lemma~\ref{lem:maxmoment} covers parity violations on the square. The distance and bit-length bounds apply more broadly, but computing the distance or extracting the constants from quantifier elimination need not be practical.

\begin{question}[Quantum and no-signalling analogues]\label{q:quantum}
For which quantum or no-signalling inflation hierarchies can one construct incompatible laws with an unbounded first rejecting order and determine its growth?
\end{question}
This question requires specifying both the hierarchy and the compatible resource model; the classical proofs here do not decide it.
