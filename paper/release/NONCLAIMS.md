# Non-claims

What the manuscript does not claim, in the numbering of the current PDF (revision of 16 September 2026). Earlier versions of this file used older numbering and paths from the author's development repository; they were removed on 16 September 2026 and remain in the git history.

- **"Inflation is incomplete."** False: the hierarchy is asymptotically complete (Navascués–Wolfe 2020), and the paper relies on it.
- **"Some incompatible law passes every order."** False and excluded by completeness. The witnesses depend on the order (`∀t ∃P_t`, never `∃P ∀t`).
- **"Compatibility is undecidable."** Not claimed. For a fixed scenario with finite alphabets, membership of a rational law is decidable (Rosset–Gisin–Wolfe with real quantifier elimination).
- **"No instance-dependent bound exists."** Not claimed. Explicit bounds exist for laws that violate a Finner inequality (Theorem 5.11), for laws that violate a cubic parity certificate on the square or the triangle (Remark 6.3), under a distance promise (Corollary 6.1), and in principle through quantifier elimination.
- **Not an explicit rejecting order for every incompatible law.** Question 7.5 remains open for laws outside the reach of Theorem 5.11, Remark 6.3 and a known distance.
- **Not tight rejecting orders from the cubic certificates.** Remark 6.3 gives an upper bound. On the W law it gives 73, while Navascués and Wolfe report rejection at order three. The certificate constants 8 and 10 are not known to be sharp.
- **Not a new incompatible example.** The W law's incompatibility is Example 2 of Wolfe–Spekkens–Fritz, and its Finner compliance is noted by Renou et al. 2019. The `1/n` mechanism of Remark 6.3 is Navascués–Wolfe 2020, Theorem 1; only the source-free constant is new.
- **Not a sharp worst-case convergence theorem.** On the square and the triangle, `H_t` is bracketed between `Ω(1/t)` and `O(t^{-1/2})` (Corollary 4.9); the exponent in `[1/2, 1]` is open. On longer cycles and on `P_5` only `Ω(t^{-2})` lower bounds are proved. The survivor constants `1/(47t)` and `1/(43t)` are not claimed optimal.
- **Not a universal distance-only condition number.** The `Θ(d^{-1/3})` law of Corollary 5.15 holds along `P_ε` only; `R_p` has first rejecting order 2 at every distance (Proposition 5.12).
- **Not an exact leading constant along `P_ε`.** Only `1/2 <= liminf <= limsup <= 4 - 2√3` (Proposition 5.13).
- **Not a runtime lower bound for compatibility algorithms.** The `2^{Θ(B)}` law of Proposition 6.2 concerns the inflation order; membership for the fixed binary triangle is decidable in time polynomial in `B` in principle.
- **Not that the AI and NW parity thresholds coincide at odd orders.** The certified brackets are identical at every certified odd order (through 9 on the square and through 13 on the triangle). Identical brackets do not establish equal thresholds, and no proof is claimed.
- **Not strictness of `I_t^AI ⊊ I_t^NW` at every order.** The separations of Proposition 4.11 hold at particular orders and rest on exact certificates. Theorem 4.10 bounds the order conversion by a factor `3/2` on the square only.
- **Not a classification beyond pair-source scenarios.** Theorem 3.2 classifies finite simple graphs with binary observations, and Remark 3.8 extends it to fixed finite observed alphabets. Scenarios in which a source is shared by three or more observers are not treated here (they are the subject of a companion paper), and neither are general causal structures with observed parents.
- **Not a quantum, no-signalling or polygon inflation theorem.**
- **Not priority or firstness.** See `PRIORITY_AUDIT.md`.
- **Sampled checks are not proofs.** The random compatible models in `artifact/certificates/beyond-finner/` test the constants of Remark 6.3; the proof is in the text.
- **Formal verification is partial, and its scope is stated.** The Lean development in this repository (`TriangleInflation/`, no `sorry`, axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` only; 113 audited declarations) proves, for binary observations and finite latent alphabets: the definitions and nesting of the three tests, soundness, the root-sink lemma, source-disjoint independence at order two, transport, exhaustion, the double-star reconstruction, the cycle and five-path witnesses at every order with their incompatibility and distance bounds, quantitative parity rigidity, local flips, and the equivalence and strictly positive witness existence of Theorem 3.2. It proves the square and triangle witnesses of Theorems 4.3 and 4.4 at `q = 1/(16t)` only (`square_linear_witness`, `triangle_linear_witness`, with `c = 5` and `c = 4`). On the triangle it proves the defect construction and Theorem 5.2 (also for arbitrary latent spaces, `no_finite_characterizing_orderM`), the fan inequalities, Proposition 5.12 in part, the finite bounds of Proposition 5.13, the triangle case of Corollary 6.1 and the triangle norm estimates of Theorem 4.8. Not formalized: rationality of the classification witnesses, Remark 3.8, the wider ranges of Theorems 4.3 and 4.4, Lemma 4.5, Corollaries 4.6 and 4.7, Theorem 4.10, Propositions 4.11 and 4.12 with Appendix B, Corollary 5.3, the limit form of Proposition 5.13, Theorem 5.14, Corollary 5.15, Proposition 6.2, Remark 6.3 and Appendix D. Nonmembership in the finite-latent compatible set is the weaker reading and does not by itself give nonmembership in the arbitrary-latent set; the arbitrary-latent form is proved in Lean only for the triangle defect family.
- **Not the strong-converse or faithfulness theorems**, which belong to a separate paper.
