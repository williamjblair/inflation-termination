# Journal targets

| rank | venue | fit | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Journal of Causal Inference | high | home of both NW 2020 and WSF 2019; the readership that posed the question; length and elementary style fit |
| 2 | Quantum | high | publishes classical and quantum inflation work (Boghiu–Wolfe–Pozas-Kerstjens; Weilenmann–Budroni–Navascués); open access; fast |
| 3 | IEEE Transactions on Information Theory | medium | if paired with the sequel (strong converse / faithfulness); the standalone theorem is short for TIT |
| 4 | Journal of Mathematical Physics | medium | hosted the bilocal inflation completeness paper; mathematical-physics framing of network causal structures |
| 5 | Annales Henri Poincaré / Communications in Mathematical Physics | low–medium | CMP hosted the quantum convergent hierarchy; the present result is elementary and classical, which argues for a causal-inference venue instead |

Recommendation: submit to Journal of Causal Inference; post to arXiv under
quant-ph with cross-lists to math.ST and cs.IT (or math.PR). Send the
specialist packet to an inflation author for the priority check first.
